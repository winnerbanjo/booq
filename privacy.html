Official Trust & Privacy Statement

Privacy Policy

At Booq (booq.africa), we build retail software and counter hardware for Nigerian merchants. We treat your shop’s sales data, inventory counts, and customer phone numbers with the highest standard of confidentiality.

Effective Date: 24 November 2026 • Jurisdiction: Federal Republic of Nigeria (NDPR / NDPA 2023)

01. Introduction & Scope

This Privacy Policy governs your use of Booq, accessible via https://booq.africa, https://booq-jet.vercel.app, and associated mobile and web point-of-sale applications operated by Booq Technologies Inc. (“Booq”, “we”, “our”, or “us”).

This policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website, apply for early merchant access, order counter hardware (such as the Booq Pulse One thermal printer), or use the Booq counter sales system.

02. Our Zero-Spam & Zero-Brokerage Promise

🔒 The Merchant Guarantee: We will never sell, rent, monetize, or trade your email, phone number, inventory data, or counter sales receipts with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

Counter sales records, stock counts, and customer details exist solely to run your store. Your data belongs entirely to your business.

03. Information We Collect

We only collect information necessary to deliver our products and services:

  • Early Access Waitlist: When you submit our early access form, we collect your email address, and optionally your shop name and merchandise category (e.g., fashion, beauty, electronics).
  • Hardware Orders: When you order hardware (such as the Booq Pulse One thermal printer), we collect your full name, phone number (for delivery dispatch & WhatsApp coordination), shop name, Nigerian state, delivery address, and payment preference.
  • Counter Point-of-Sale Data: When using Booq POS, transactions (item, quantity, naira price, time, receipt ID) and inventory counts are maintained on your device and synchronized securely to your merchant dashboard.
  • Technical Telemetry: Anonymized crash logs and aggregate browser metrics to ensure our application runs fast on Android, iOS, and desktop browsers across Nigerian mobile networks.

04. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly to:

  • Notify you when Booq is ready for your shop onboarding.
  • Fulfill and dispatch physical hardware orders to your store address across Lagos, Abuja, Port Harcourt, and nationwide.
  • Power instant receipt generation, shelf stock decrements, and sales dashboard reconciliation.
  • Provide merchant customer support via WhatsApp or email when you request technical assistance.
  • Maintain platform security and protect against fraudulent transactions.

05. Third-Party Service Providers

We partner with a limited number of verified technical infrastructure providers:

  • Payment Gateways: When processing online payments, transactions are handled by licensed Nigerian payment processors (such as Paystack or Flutterwave). We do not store raw debit card numbers or banking passwords on our servers.
  • Logistics Partners: For hardware deliveries, customer shipping information (name, phone, address) is shared with verified dispatch riders and courier partners solely to execute package delivery.
  • Hosting & Edge Infrastructure: Encrypted hosting via Vercel and secure cloud storage to serve the fastest experience on 3G and 4G connections.

06. Data Security & Local-First Resilience

Retail counters in Nigeria frequently face network downtime and power outages. Booq is engineered with a local-first architecture:

Sales records and stock balances are written instantly to local offline storage before being queued for cloud sync. All data in transit between your phone and our cloud servers is encrypted using industry-standard TLS 1.3 encryption.

07. Your Rights & Data Control

In compliance with the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA 2023) and the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR), you retain full rights over your data:

  • Right to Access: You may request a copy of all personal and merchant data associated with your account.
  • Right to Rectification: You may update or correct your shop details or phone number at any time.
  • Right to Erasure (Deletion): You may request complete deletion of your waitlist record, order history, or merchant account.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You may unsubscribe from launch announcements at any time with a single click or email.

08. Children’s Privacy

Booq is intended exclusively for retail businesses, store operators, and adult merchants. We do not knowingly collect personal data from individuals under 18 years of age.

09. Updates to This Policy

As Booq introduces new hardware accessories and capabilities (such as barcode scanners, thermal receipt syncing, and team roles), we may update this Privacy Policy. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
