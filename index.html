Privacy at Booq

We respect your shop and your inbox. When you request early access to Booq, we collect your email address and optional shop name solely to notify you when the app is ready for onboarding.

We do not sell, rent, or distribute merchant emails to third parties. Emails are never shared with or sent to third-party analytics services.
